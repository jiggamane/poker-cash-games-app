Open a night and it becomes the whole table — your name bolded, nobody highlighted. Two plans, two lists: Regular shows every night in the group, Free shows the last three you played.

The foot line has three states One line at the foot of the results, and it is the only place the screen says whether the numbers can be trusted. Settled (green check): “Every stack was counted and the deductions are in, so these results are final.” Not settled yet (amber): “Before the bill and the kitty. Each result changes once the deductions are worked out.” Short (red): “Short by $120. The counted stacks are less than what went in. Nothing is final until it balances.”
The em-dash means two different things In 1A it means he was not there — the row is legible, the caption says who did play, and the night is still openable. In 1B it means the app is not showing him — dimmer text, a padlock instead of a chevron, and the row does not open. Same glyph, opposite cause, so the treatments have to be distinguishable at a glance.
Open questions Can a Free player open a night he played but which has fallen outside his three? Right now the row is locked, which means his own history disappears behind a paywall — defensible, but worth a decision. And on 1A, should a missed night be openable at all: he was not in it, but the result is the group’s, not his.
