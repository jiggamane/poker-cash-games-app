Decisionswhat I assumed, and what only you can answer
Assumed · say if wrong Three standings, not three roles Admin runs nights and edits everything. Member has the app and reads. Name only has no app and is seated, counted and settled exactly like a member. Standing is per club, not per person — the same human can be admin in one group and a name in another. Naming comes first, the invite second The admin adds a player by name and they immediately play, count and settle like anyone else. Only then can a link be sent, and it is that player’s link: opening it hands them the name they already have, along with every night of theirs in the book. So there is no generic group invite, no pending-members list, and “invited” is a badge on a roster row. The group owns the money rules; a night borrows them GR8 is what a new night copies. The house rules sheet inside a night can override any of it for that night alone, and changing the group default never reaches back into a running or settled night.
Open · needs your call 1 · Can a club have two admins? The single-manager decision covers who controls a night. It does not say whether two people can administer the club between nights, or how admin is handed over. GR7's "hand over admin first" note assumes one admin at a time and a transfer step that is not drawn. 2 · Leaving and deleting Both rows are drawn, neither confirm sheet is. Three questions: can you leave with an unsettled debt, does deleting a club destroy nights other people played in, and does anyone but the last admin get to delete at all. 3 · The book The navigation doc lists a club-level "book — month / all time" as a push off the club. It is not on GR1: club home has My nights, which is the reader's own history. If the group needs a shared ledger of every night, it is a fifth row and a screen nobody has drawn. 4 · Where My stats lives G4 is confirmed as a screen and classified as a push off the club, but GR1 has no row for it — My nights leads to the same history. Either My stats becomes the row and the nights list sits inside it, or the two merge. Pick one; the section cannot ship with both routes.
