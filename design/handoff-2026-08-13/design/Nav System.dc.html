Two chromes, one rule for choosing between them

No tab bar. Screens you navigate to are pushed and carry a round back button on the title line. Screens you open to do one thing — a player, an amount, a setting — are sheets over what you were looking at, and dismiss downward. The test: if a screen has a job that finishes, it is a sheet. If it is a place, it is a push.

Which is which Push: the club (root, no chevron), Tonight, My nights, a past night, My stats, Players, Money rules, Settings, the book. Sheet: a player, an amount keypad, a picker, a rule editor, a bill, a correction, the table-admin drawer, every confirm. Rule of thumb: if the screen ends with a Save, an Add or an Apply, it is a sheet.
Depth, and the one hard limit Pushes go three deep at most: club → a night → nothing further. Sheets go two deep at most: a player sheet may raise an amount sheet, and that is the floor. A sheet never pushes; if a flow needs a third level, it replaces the sheet’s content in place and keeps the same close.
