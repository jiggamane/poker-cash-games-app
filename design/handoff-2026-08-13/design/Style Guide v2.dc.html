Two themes, no accent, one loud button

Everything here is drawn at the size it ships at. The system has no brand hue on purpose: fill and weight carry the actions, which leaves green and red free to mean only one thing — money won and money lost.

Study · how much colour on money

Four intensities, same settle-up list

Left is what ships today — colour on the figure only. Each step to the right spends more of it. Adopted: M1. It keeps the black-and-white calm while still letting a win or a loss register at a glance, and it is now the row standard across the app. M2 is good if the settle-up screen specifically should feel like a verdict. M3 is too loud for a screen people read every week — colour stops meaning "money" and starts meaning "alarm".
