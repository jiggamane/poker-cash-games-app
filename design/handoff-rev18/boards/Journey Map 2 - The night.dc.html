Every frame was checked against its container in 09-navigation.md and the later specs before being placed; 25 were drawn in the wrong chrome and have been corrected, each marked in green with what changed. 04

Run the night

Three to five hours of one person logging movements of money as they happen: buy-ins, rebuys, cash-outs, corrections. This is the screen the app exists for.
What everyone else seesThe same list, read-only, their own position first and second-person phrasing on their own entries. No controls, and none greyed out.
Where it goes wrongIt runs offline in somebody’s kitchen on bad wifi. Nothing in the drawn set shows a queued or unsynced entry.
