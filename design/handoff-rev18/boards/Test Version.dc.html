Test version · one club, no accounts

Start a night, add people as they turn up, settle

The build that can be tested on one table in one evening. One fixed club, so nothing in the nav ever asks which club you are in. Nobody signs in — the admin's phone holds the night, and players are names typed at the table as they arrive. The money is the full model: buy-ins and rebuys, the piggy bank, the bill and the host fee, all resolved at the close.

Logic to apply in code

What the missing club selection, the missing accounts and the missing roster change about the rules.
Who anybody is A player is a name and nothing else. Typing a name creates them for this night; typing a name the app has seen before offers it as a suggestion and reuses that record, so twelve nights of Ivo add up. Two people with one name is the one collision the test build accepts: the second is saved as typed, Ivo (2), and can be renamed later. There is one club and one device. No sign-in, no invite, no watcher — everything happens on the admin's phone, passed around the table if someone wants to look.
Opening a night Home resolves to one of three states from the club's own history: a live night wins, then no nights at all, then idle. There is no club to choose, so the root never asks a question before showing the card. T4 asks for four numbers and remembers them: buy-in, piggy bank per player, host fee, and whether there is a bill. Second night onward they arrive pre-filled from the last night, which is the only thing Settings changes. Confirming T4 creates the night with nobody in it. Backing out of T4 creates nothing.
Getting people in One action, used all night: name, then amount. The first entry is a buy-in; every later one for the same name is a rebuy. Nothing distinguishes a late arrival from an early one, which is the point of dropping the roster. The amount is pre-filled with tonight's buy-in and can be typed over — short buys and double buys are ordinary entries, not exceptions. Every entry carries its own time, and the last one can be undone from the table for as long as the night is open.
The money, and the close On the table = buy-ins + rebuys − cash-outs. The piggy bank is a flat amount per player, counted at the close, not taken out of the buy-in. The host fee is one amount off the top. The bill is entered at the close and split evenly across everyone who played. Rules stay editable until the first cash-out. After that the piggy bank and fee are frozen for the night, because changing them would rewrite money already paid out. T9 balances counted stacks against money in. Equal — it settles. Short or over — the amber door: settle anyway, with the difference recorded against the night.
