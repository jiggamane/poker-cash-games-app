E1

End the night · confirm

Sheet (Chrome B) over Tonight · dark + light

The off-ramp that stops a night closing while chips are still on the table. It appears the moment End this poker night is held on the Tonight dock and any seated player is uncounted.

  • Title is a count, not a warning: “4 players still have chips”. The number is the uncounted seated players, and the sheet does not appear at all when it is zero.
  • One row per uncounted player: 36px round initial (`#16161A / #F4F4F6` fill, `700 14px` muted initial), name `600 17px` over `in for $1,500 · not counted` at `400 12.5px` muted, hairline under every row.
  • Count them up (filled, full width) pushes E2. Not yet dismisses back to Tonight and changes nothing.
  • Errata — build to Chrome B, not to this drawing. The sheet is drawn at `radius 24px` with a `38 × 4` grabber and a flat `rgba(10,10,11,.78)` dim. `09-navigation.md` is FINAL and wins: `radius 26px 26px 0 0`, `38 × 5` grabber, and the real Tonight screen behind at `opacity: .32`.

E2

Count up

Push (Chrome A) · step 1 of 3 · dark + light

A count for every seated player, drawn mid-count so the two list states are both visible. The header card is neutral until the counted total matches what is on the table.

  • Header card: `COUNTED` eyebrow (`700 11px`, `.1em`), then `$2,610` at `800 30px` with of $2,880 inline at `17px` muted, and `2 TO GO` pushed right at `700 13px`. It is `#16161A` hairline — no green, no red, until the count balances.
  • Three list groups, each under a `700 12px` `.1em` uppercase label: Still to count · 2 (value is an em dash, pencil glyph), Done · 3 (value in foreground, green check), Already gone (whole row muted, `cashed out 23:15 · in $500`, no glyph — not tappable).
  • Apply the money rules is blocked while any stack is uncounted: `#16161A` fill, muted label, `inset 0 0 0 2px #0A0A0B`, with the reason stated under it (“Two stacks still to count.”).
  • “See where everyone stands” is an underlined text link above the primary → E2b. `1 of 3` sits at the right of the title row on every step of the flow.

Where everyone stands

Push off E2 · dark + light

A read-only look at the table mid-count. It exists so nobody has to guess while the counting drags on, and it is careful to say that nothing has been taken off yet.

  • Ranked by gross result, counted players only, rank numeral in a fixed left column. Row: name over `in $500 · out $2,120`, result right in green/red.
  • Uncounted players sit in their own group below with an em dash, unranked.
  • Two sentences carry the whole point of the screen: the lede (“Nothing has come off the table yet — the bill and the piggy bank land at the next step”) and the footnote (“Ranks are provisional until every stack is counted”). Keep both verbatim.
  • One action: Back to the count. Nothing on this screen writes.

E3

Deductions

Push (Chrome A) · step 2 of 3 · dark + light

Every rule, itemised per person, with the total leaving the table stated at the top. The tallest screen in the app — it scrolls.

  • Header card states the money and where it goes in one line: `$296` at display size over “$120 back to Marek, $50 to Lena · $126 to the piggy bank”.
  • One block per rule: rule name and total on a title row, the split sentence beneath, then a per-player row with the amount and a pencil. The piggy bank block shows the working (`5% of $1,620` → `$81`) because a percentage is not checkable otherwise.
  • Everyone after deductions is a preview, not a result. Dashed frame, `PREVIEW` tag, columns GROSS / BILL / BACK / PIGGY BANK / NET, and it recomputes as any figure above changes. Losers show gross and net only — both rules charge winners.
  • A reimbursement is a `+` in the BACK column, never a separate row: Marek `460 −57 +120 −23 = +$500`.
  • Footnote “Provisional until you settle. Tap any figure above to change it.” — every amount on this screen is editable in place.

Deductions · before the bill

Same screen, bill not yet entered · dark + light

The same screen when the bill has not been added. It is the common case, not an edge case: the bar tab usually arrives after the count.

  • The bill block keeps its position and its rows and shows em dashes, with “Nothing on the bill yet. Add it and the split appears here.” The block is never hidden.
  • Header card reads `$126` — “Only the piggy bank so far · the bill is not in yet”.
  • In the preview, BILL and BACK are em dashes and NET is the piggy bank-only figure. Nets differ from E3 by exactly the bill split.
  • Footnote swaps to “Waiting on the bill. The count fills in as the figures above are set.” The primary is not blocked — a night with no bill is a legitimate settle.

E4

Settle up

Push (Chrome A) · step 3 of 3 · dark + light

The shortest set of transfers that clears the night, then the night’s net per player. This is the screen the table reads out loud.

  • Transfer row: payer, arrow glyph, payee, amount right. The piggy bank is a payee like anyone else (`Tomáš → The piggy bank $126`).
  • The count is stated in words above the list (“Six transfers clear the night. The piggy bank is set aside for the group.”) so a wrong number is visible immediately.
  • Night’s net below, best first, in the green/red pair. These are the after-deduction nets from E3, unchanged.
  • Edit sits top right. Editing rules: a debtor cannot receive, Save stays blocked until the set balances, Reset restores the generated set. See `04-money-math.md`.
  • Close the session freezes the night; Share and Export sit under it as a secondary pair.

E5

Settle up · out of balance

Sheet replacing E4 in the flow · dark + light

The second off-ramp. When counted chips do not equal the money that went in, this replaces the settle-up rather than showing a warning on it.

  • The gap is stated twice — as a tag (`Off by $150`) and as a sentence naming both figures and both likely causes: “$4,500 went in, $4,350 was counted out. Someone’s stack is short, or a buy-in was never written down.”
  • Every counted player is listed with `in · out` and their result, each row a pencil into that count. Fixing is done here, not somewhere else.
  • Settle the night is disabled (the blocked token pair from E2) until the difference is zero or written off. Fix is the live action.
  • Third route, stated in the footnote: write the difference off to the piggy bank. That creates a real ledger entry — it is not a dismissal.
  • Data note: this frame is drawn from a different sample night ($4,500 in, five players). Do not read its roster against E2–E4; it exists to show the state.

E7

Payments

Off the settled night · dark + light

Settling and paying are separate. The book closes at the table; the money moves over the following week, and this is the only screen that tracks it.

  • One row per transfer from E4: payer → payee, state under it (`marked paid 00:19` or `waiting`), amount right, and a Mark paid button on waiting rows only.
  • Only the host marks a payment paid. Players and watchers see the same list read-only — it is bookkeeping, not a claim a player files.
  • Running total above the action: `$760 of $1,350 still owed`. Nudge the table is the single action.
  • Nothing here changes the night’s result. A settled night stays settled whether or not the cash has moved.
