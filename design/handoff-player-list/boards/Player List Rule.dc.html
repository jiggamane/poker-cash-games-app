What each screen adds
N8 is the plainest case — the finished slab is there only so the host cannot cash the same person out twice, so it carries the result and no affordance. E1 is a sheet, so the rule has to survive a 36px avatar in the active row: the avatar sits before the name, the slab never gets one.
The one inversion
E2b is the only screen where the finished group comes first, because the screen is a ranking and only a final result can be ranked. The active group sits below it, unranked, with an em dash in the same fixed 16px numeral column. Cashed-out-earlier players rank alongside counted ones — their result is equally final.
Two things to decide
E1 previously listed only uncounted players; the cashed-out slab is new here, and it answers "who is already done" without leaving the sheet. E2b's group label was counted only; ranking six instead of three follows from treating both finished states alike. Both are proposals — say the word and I will pull either back.
One rule · every screen with a mixed player list

Active rows, finished slabs

Taken from 1a, the Count up reference, and applied to Tonight. A player who still has money on the table is a plain hairline row with a full-strength name and something to do at the right edge. A player who is finished — counted or cashed out — is a tinted slab: name in muted, the fact on the same line, the signed result at the right, nothing tappable. The slab is what says "settled", so the section label stays short and never has to carry it.

Active row
Hairline under, padding 10 0, no fill, 44 tall. One line, gap 9: name 17/700 in text, then the money-in fact 13/400 in dim beside it — never stacked under the name. Right edge holds the affordance: em dash + 17px pencil, or a buy-in figure 19/700 and chevron.
Finished slab
Surface fill, radius 8, padding 8 14, 5 between slabs, one line, gap 9 — 39 tall, deliberately under 44 because nothing here is tappable. Name 17/700 muted, fact 13/400 dim beside it, signed result 19/700 in win/loss at the right. No glyph, no chevron, no hairline.
Group order and labels
Active group always first, then finished groups newest work first. Label 11/700 tracking .1em muted, padding 10 0 4 (0 above the first), name and count only — result before deductions is no longer needed, the slab carries it. Results and Settle up have no division: everyone there is finished.
