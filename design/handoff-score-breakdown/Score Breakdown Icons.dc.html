Turn 4 · drawn out, not sketched

Four for the piggy, three for food

The earlier set was thin on detail — open ends, two-tine forks, shapes that stop before they resolve. These carry the marks that finish them: a tie on the bag, a snout and tail on the pig, a rim on the coin, a third tine on the fork. Still 1.7 stroke in a 20 px box.

4A
Bag of money Gathered top, tie band, mark on the body. Widest silhouette of the four, so it holds its own at the end of the row.
4B
Piggy bank Ellipse body, round snout, closed ear, slot, tail and two legs. The most literal of the set — check it at 15 px below.
4C
Percent share Approved. Rim, then the mark. Right glyph if the piggy is a cut of the pot rather than a flat contribution.
4D
Dollar coin Same coin, cash instead of a share. Reads at any size; risks reading as a generic money glyph rather than the piggy.
4E
Fork & knife, filled out Approved. Three tines gathered into a neck, and the knife now has a spine and a belly instead of one bent line.
4F
Plate Rim and well, nothing else. Quietest option, but two concentric circles now appear twice in the set.
4G
Hot bowl One closed shape with steam. Reads as a takeaway order — which is usually what the food line is.

Turn 2 · replacing the pig · settled

The jar takes the fifth slot — for now

The pig is out: the jar from turn 1 took the piggy bank, and drinks moved from a tumbler to a cocktail glass. The jar was itself replaced in turn 4 by the percent coin; the cocktail glass stayed. The four candidates below are kept as the record of what was weighed.

2A
Vault Squares off against four round-ish neighbours, so it separates fast in a row. Reads “held back” rather than “saved up”.
2B
Coin slot Two paths. Keeps the slot — the only part of the pig that carried meaning — and drops the animal. Risk: close to a plain chip.
2C
Locked box Padlock body with a shackle. Strongest “not yours yet” signal of the four; heaviest ink at 15 px.
2D
Kitty pot The cinched bag. Closest in feel to the jar already on the board, and the word players actually use at the table.
