  1. Pick a deck on the control: Call or fold, My move, or Bomb pot.
  2. Deal. The card turns face down, the light runs along the chips and slows, and the card turns over on the answer. About a second, with a tap of the haptics on each step and a firmer one on the answer. With reduced motion it simply turns over.
  3. Deal again draws afresh. Nothing is kept between deals and nothing is recorded.
  4. Bomb pot · Edit opens your sizes. Type a number on the keypad, Add, remove with the bin, and Save. The close throws the changes away.

What this draft decides

Where it livesPoker tools, the fourth tile, in the half of the second row that was empty. Tonight’s Poker tools sheet draws the same tiles, so it is one tap from a live game.
What it isOne pushed screen, /randomiser (Chrome A: back, nothing top-right). It is a place you stay in while the hand plays, not something you confirm, so it is not a sheet.
Three decksA segmented control: Call or fold (two answers), My move (Call, Fold, Raise, All in), Bomb pot (your sizes). It opens on the last deck used.
The answer is a cardFace down until you deal. Deal turns it over on the answer and lights its chip below, so you see both the answer and what it was drawn from. Each answer has its own suit; the two that put chips in are red.
Fair, and says so“1 in 2 each” / “1 in 4 each” over the chips. Every deal is independent and drawn from the phone’s secure random source, so the same answer can come twice running.
Bomb pot sizes are yoursMultiples of the big blind (the Bomb pot booster states the ante that way). Added and removed on their own sheet, which ends in Save. At least two before Deal is live.
Nothing is recordedLike every poker tool, it never reads or writes the ledger. One option below would let it read tonight’s blinds.

Open questions

Answers to these change the build; the first one changes the server.

Where the sizes are savedThis draft keeps them on the phone. Saving them with the group instead means every phone at the table deals from the same list, and they could sit beside the group’s table rules, but it needs a server change. Recommendation: phone first, group later if asked for.
Should Call or fold and My move be editable too?Drawn as fixed, since the request asked for user variants on the bomb pot only.
WeightsAll answers are equally likely. A table might want All in rarer, or ×5 rarer than ×2. Not drawn; adding the same size twice is not allowed.
Show the amount at tonight’s blindsFrom Tonight, the bomb pot card could add “$30 each at $5 / $10”. That would make this the one tool that reads the night (blinds only, through packages/core). Drawn as an option.
Half sizes and named sizes×1.5, or “double board ×2”. This draft takes whole numbers from 1 to 20.
The tile’s drawingA draft: a tossed red chip. It needs a pass on the icon board to sit with the other three.
