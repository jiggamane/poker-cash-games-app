The Poker Club · visual study · 9 November 2026

A face for the Thursday game

Why the app reads as a utility, three directions that would change that, and the one to take. Every phone below is drawn on the app’s own seeded night, settled by the engine’s rules, so the figures are real to the dollar.

The short answer

The app is disciplined and almost anonymous. Its rules protect legibility everywhere, and none of them gives it a face.

The icon is Expo’s placeholder. Every heading is the same 800-weight system font. The greys have no temperature. The end of the night, the one moment people care about, is drawn exactly like the middle of it.

Colour can’t fix this, because colour already has a job: green and red are money and mean nothing else. That rule is the best idea in the design and it should stay. Personality has to come from the other channels: typeface, material, shape and motion.

Recommendation: Direction B, Card Room, reached in three steps. The first step is Direction A’s groundwork, which any direction needs and which ships almost entirely through tokens.ts.

01 · Today

What it looks like now

Eight reasons it reads as a utility

Nobody designed the front door

The icon and splash are the Expo template’s. On a home screen the app looks like a developer build, before anyone has opened it.

apps/mobile/assets/icon.png

One voice at one volume

The type scale has 68 roles and one register. Titles, tile names, card titles and figures are all SF Pro at 800. When everything is heavy, size is the only hierarchy left, which is how system settings look.

11 roles at 800 in tokens.ts, 29 more set inline

Greys without temperature

#0A0A0B, #16161A, #26262B: neutral, slightly blue, the colours of a phone’s own chrome. A home game happens under warm light, on wood, with paper money.

darkTheme.ground / surface / raised

Every container is the same box

Tiles, the on-the-table card, the cashed-out row, the dock and the stats card share one fill and one radius family. With no material language, a screen reads as boxes, and the pure-white primary button is the most stock element of all.

surface #16161A at radius 14, everywhere

The night has no ending

The settled night is the emotional peak: who won, who pays. It is drawn in the same type, on the same ground, as the live list. Nothing marks the night as finished, and nothing is made to be sent to the group chat.

/settled, docs/sharing-formats.md

People are only text

The group is the product, and players are names in a list. Initials appear only in the sheets for picking people. On Tonight, count up, the settled night and who pays whom, a person is a word.

Avatar.tsx: new-night, pick, pass-game, hand-over

Back-office vocabulary

Uppercase tracked grey labels on every section (STILL PLAYING · 5, COUNTED · 1), a chevron on every row, a drawer called Table admin. Each one is reasonable. Together they are the look of an internal tool.

type.sectionLabel, type.label

Poker is absent

Outside the odds calculator, nothing on screen says cards, chips or table. The most recognisable material in the subject’s world is unused, so the app could be splitting a restaurant bill.

PlayingCard.tsx renders on /odds only

What every direction keeps

Colour is moneyGreen won, red lost, bone left the table. Nothing else is coloured. Each direction below obeys this, and two of them had to move a colour to keep obeying it.
Figures are tabular and bigRead at arm’s length across a table, never shrunk to fit. Every figure in every mockup is tabular at today’s sizes or larger.
Chrome A and Chrome BPushed screens have a round back button and nothing top-right; sheets have a grabber and a close. The mockups keep both.
The copyEvery string in the mockups is shipped copy, except the few listed under each direction.
Contrast floorsEvery text colour proposed here clears 4.5:1 on the surface it sits on. The ratios are printed beside each swatch.

03 · Direction A

Sharpened

The same app, finished. Keep the system face and the structure; give the type two registers, warm the greys, and let the rules and washes measure something.

Risk: lowEngineering: tokens + 4 componentsNo new dependency
{{A_PHONES}}

What changes

  • Two weights carry the hierarchy. 700 for titles and the hero figure, 500 to 600 for everything read in passing. 800 is gone. The screen gets quieter and the one big number gets louder.
  • The neutrals warm by a step. #0E0D0C in place of #0A0A0B; the same lightness ladder, the same contrast, a different temperature.
  • Section labels go to sentence case. “Still playing 5” with the count a tone quieter, in place of STILL PLAYING · 5.
  • The hero figure leaves its box. One object per screen is raised. On Tonight that is the dock, the thing your thumb is on; the figure stands on the ground at 62pt.
  • Rules measure. The lit part of the line under each player is their share of the money in play. On the settled night the existing win and loss wash runs to the length of the result. Decoration becomes a chart without adding anything.
  • Home becomes four lines. The tile grid’s icon-in-a-circle is the most template-looking element in the app. This reopens the owner’s 25 October tile decision (N6), so it is offered, not assumed.

04 · Direction B

Card Room

The back room of a club at eleven at night. Walnut dark, card-stock ivory, an engraved serif for the few figures that matter, and playing-card material for the objects you hold.

RecommendedRisk: mediumEngineering: expo-font + tokens + ~10 components
{{B_PHONES}}

What changes

  • A display face with one job. Gloock, a high-contrast serif with the cut of a card index, sets titles and the single hero figure on a screen. Everything a person scans (names, amounts in rows, times) stays in Figtree, tabular, at today’s sizes.
  • Walnut and ivory. The ground is warm without being brown, and text is card-stock ivory, which is gentler than white in a dim room and still clears 15:1.
  • The things you hold are cards. Start a session, Rebuy and the night’s result are ivory stock with ink on it and a spade in two corners. The app already does this once, with home’s inverted live card; here it becomes the rule.
  • Every person gets a seat token. Initials in the serif inside a chip edge, wherever a name appears: Tonight, count up, the settled night, who pays whom. Neutral, never coloured.
  • The night ends on a result card. The top line of the settled night, framed so it could be sent to the group chat as it is. It would be the first thing in the app made for someone who wasn’t at the table.
  • Two colours move to keep the rule. Bone off-table money sits too close to ivory text, so it becomes brass (#C9AE7E). Amber “pending” would then sit too close to brass, so it becomes a clearer orange (#F09A3E).

What it costs

expo-font (in SDK 58’s manifest, runs in Expo Go) with two OFL faces. This reverses the decision at the head of tokens.ts that no web font ships, and in doing so closes the gap that note names: Android falls back to Roboto today. Figtree is already the handoff’s stand-in face, so the widths ui-check --figtree measures still hold. Every board needs recutting.

The risk

Kitsch. Suits, felt and gold are one step from a casino app. The guard rails: black suits only, and only in card corners; no felt green, which would fight the win colour; no gold; the serif never below 28pt, where its hairlines would shimmer on a dark ground.

New strings

None. The result card uses the settled night’s meta (“$5,000 in play · 3h 15m · 6 players”) and the top name; it needs a decision on whether it shows only the top line or the top three.

05 · Direction C

Ledger

The first handoff’s own sentence: “This app is that piece of paper, done properly.” Ruled paper, ink rather than black, figures in a mono, and the conventions that make a column of money trustworthy.

Risk: medium–highEngineering: expo-font + tokens + width re-measureLight-first
{{C_PHONES}}

What changes

  • Light leads. Paper is the default and dark is the carbon copy.
  • Figures move to DM Mono. A column of money reads like a column in a book, and every digit, comma and dollar sign takes the same cell.
  • Times sit in the margin, as dates do in a ledger: when each player sat down, when Dana left.
  • Columns close. A single rule above a total and a double rule under it. “5 seated 4,500” adds up the column and agrees with the headline above it, which is the reconciliation the app already does, made visible.
  • The settled night is stamped. One mark, once, when the night closes.

What it costs

Mono figures are wider than SF’s tabular figures, and the comma and dollar sign take a full cell each, so every width budget in tokens.ts (the 147pt “$99.9M” that sets moneyMaxFontScale) has to be measured again. Light-first also changes the default theme.

The risk

It reads as accounting software, which is the opposite of what this study was asked for. It is the most faithful to the product’s idea and the least warm. Its best detail, the double rule, travels to any direction.

New strings

Column heads “sat”, “in”, “left”, “net”; the deduction lines “Andro, Lena fronted” and “Radka holds”; the stamp’s date.

08 · Recommendation

Take Card Room, in three steps

Card Room is the only direction that gives the app a face without spending the colour rule or the night-time comfort to get it. Its risk is taste, and taste can be held with rules, which this project is good at writing. A and C are better as donors: A’s groundwork is step one, and C’s double rule belongs under the settled night’s total in any direction.

Step 1

Groundwork, from A

A real icon and splash. Two type registers in place of 800 everywhere. Warm neutrals. Sentence-case section labels. One raised object per screen.

All of it lands in tokens.ts and a handful of shared components. Per CLAUDE.md that makes it an app-wide change: one session, nothing else in flight, and a small board recut first so check:ui has something true to compare against.

Step 2

The material, from B

Bundle Gloock and Figtree through expo-font. Add one display role to the type scale, used by titles and hero figures only. Make Start a session, Rebuy and the result card ivory stock. Give every name a seat token, through the existing Avatar.tsx.

The boards are the pixel source, so this starts as a handoff request to design and becomes code after the cut, as every screen here has.

Step 3

The moments

Three events get motion and a haptic, and nothing else does: a rebuy lands (the token flips), a stack is counted (the arrival animation that already exists), and the night settles (the result card deals in). expo-haptics is in SDK 58’s manifest and not yet installed.

Then the result card is the candidate for the share image. docs/sharing-formats.md says that image needs a board before it needs code, and no board draws one yet.

09 · Left out

Considered and set aside

A felt-green groundPoker’s most obvious material, and it puts the win colour on green. +$1,620 would stop standing out.
Red suitsHearts and diamonds are red, and red already means a loss. Card Room uses spades, and only in corners.
A brand hueA purple, blue or gold for “brand” would be the one colour people learn to ignore, and then they would ignore the next one too. The token file says why in its first paragraph.
Accounting parentheses(1,230) for a loss is correct in a ledger and unreadable to most people at a table. Ledger keeps the minus sign.
A colour per playerTempting for seat tokens. It collides with the money colours and fails colour-blind players. Tokens are told apart by initials.
Glass and blur throughoutLovely over a photo, a contrast hazard over a list of figures. The sheet scrim is the only place blur could earn its keep.
VegasNeon, gradients and gold trim. A home game is a kitchen table, and the people at it are friends, not customers.
