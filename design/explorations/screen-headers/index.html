The header update, screen by screen

Only the parts that change. Each pair shows the top of a screen as it is today and with the new header. Everything below the fade stays as it is. Pick the base: the app as it is in code, in either theme, or one of round two’s directions, Chip Rack or Fresh Deck.

The new header is the Rail: the head of the screen becomes one raised panel that runs up under the status bar and ends in a rounded edge. Its contents sit on it, and the list starts on the ground below. That is what fills the empty top. Lamp is the lighter option: same layout, with the ground under the header lit.
