The top of the screen

The 9 November study gave each direction a face, but kept one header for all three: a back button, a title and a timer, then a line of grey, on flat ground. The rows below it have character. The header doesn't, and the ground behind it reads as empty space. Four ways to fix that, drawn on the same seeded night. Only the header changes; every row underneath is the study's, unchanged.

The short answer

Make the header and the figure one object. Today they're two things with a gap between them, and that gap is what reads as empty.

Recommendation: H2 Rail, with H1’s lamp and grain on the ground below it. The rail turns the top third of the screen into a surface, lit and textured, with an edge, so the list visibly sits beneath it. In Card Room the edge is stitched like the padded rail of a card table, the one ornament here a player would recognise. The layout is the same as today’s, so no screen has to be measured again.

H3 is the bolder option and worth arguing for: Tonight is never what a host opens the screen to read, and $4,500 is. H4 is the safe one. It fixes the cramped title row for good, but it adds height and leaves the ground as empty as before.

H1

Lamp

Same layout. The ground gets a light source: a warm pool above the title with grain in it, fading out by the second row. The top of the screen is lit and the list sits in shade, like a lamp hung over a table.

What it fixesThe empty ground. The page now has depth and some material to it, at no cost in layout.
What it doesn’tThe header is still the same three items. It reads as better lighting on the same screen, not a new screen.
In CPaper has no lamp, so the head of the sheet is printed instead: faint ruled lines with a tint, fading into the page.
CostOne background image behind Screen.tsx. Grain is a 160-point tile. No new strings.
H2

Rail recommended

The title, the role line and the figure share one raised panel. It runs up under the status bar and ends in a rounded edge, and the list sits on the ground below it. The figure gives up its own card, so the gap goes too.

What it fixesBoth complaints. The header is now an object rather than a row of text, and there's no strip of empty ground left between the header and the figure.
In BA stitched seam runs round the rail’s edge, like the padded rail of a card table. Card stock at low alpha, not brass: brass means off-table money in B.
In CThe rail is the head of a ledger page: a white sheet ending in a double rule, with the margin running through it.
CostA header slot in Screen.tsx that a screen can put its hero into. Tonight and the settled night use it; plain lists keep a short rail with just the title. Same heights as today. No new strings.
H3

Figure first

The title shrinks to a navigation-bar title and the money becomes the header: centred, larger than anything else in the app, set on a chip's edge drawn at header size. It's the same ring as the seat tokens and the live tag.

What it fixesHierarchy. Today the largest thing on screen is the word Tonight. Here it's the number the host is checking.
What it costsThe title row’s settled layout (docs/tonight-title-row.md) and the left-aligned grid every other screen shares. A centred hero is a second header style to keep consistent.
Settled nightThe date becomes the bar title, and the winner card sits centred on the ring. The meta line and the view control are unchanged.
StringsNone new. The meta line puts the existing “$5,000 in play” and “5 seated · 1 out” on one line, joined by a dot.
H4

Large title

The back button and the timer get a row of their own. The title takes the full width below them, under the group's crest and name. In B the crest appears again as a watermark, cropped off the top-right corner.

What it fixesThe cramped title row. docs/tonight-title-row.md found that a full-width title is the one layout that fits at 375 points and at large text sizes.
What it doesn’tThe empty ground. The watermark helps a little in B. In A and C the header is just taller.
CostAbout 40 points of header, which is most of one player row on Tonight.
Strings⚠ The eyebrow shows the group name (The Thursday game) on Tonight. That's data rather than new copy, but no board puts it on this screen. Flagged, not decided.
