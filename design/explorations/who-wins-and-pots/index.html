Who wins, and Side pots

Two new poker tools beside the calculator. Who wins reads the cards on the table and says whose hand is best right now, what it is and the five cards that make it: no percentages. Side pots takes the same cards plus each player’s stack and splits a multi-way all in into its pots, says who takes each one, and what everybody ends the hand with. Drawn in the look approved on 22 November (the full board’s kit). No app code has changed. Approve or redirect here first.

Side pots

everyone all in, pot by pot
  1. Tap a place for a card, on a seat or the board. The deck comes up at the foot; tap a card and the next empty place is outlined. Done puts the deck away.
  2. Tap a dealt card to take it back.
  3. Who wins answers on the ink as soon as two seats have both cards, and again on every street you deal.
  4. Side pots: tap a stack to type it on the keypad, then Done. The result, hand by hand, appears under the seats once the stacks and cards are in.
  5. Add a player goes up to nine.

What this draft decides

Tiles and orderPoker tools reads Rulings, Calculator, Boosters, Who wins, Side pots (owner, 27 November). Who wins is five framed cards lying on the table; Side pots is two pots side by side, the bigger on the right.
Two tools, two tilesWho wins and Side pots sit on Poker tools beside the calculator, so Tonight’s Poker tools sheet (ToolTiles) gets them too. Each is a push, /who-wins and /side-pots: a place you stay in while the hand plays out, with nothing to confirm.
The calculator’s tableSame board, same seats, same deck as a keyboard at the foot, same Add a player and New hand. A seat counts once both its cards are in. Up to nine seats.
Who wins: the answer on the inkUnder the title: Ahead, Tied, Winner or Split, the street, the winning hole cards, and the hand. No percentages anywhere. Before the flop it compares the two hole cards; from the flop the best five.
The five cards, alwaysFrom the flop each seat shows its best five so far: the cards that make the hand full, kickers faded. A kicker that decided the hand is not faded, and is named, as the calculator names it.
Side pots: stacks in the seatEach seat carries its stack at the right, typed on the calculator’s keypad with Done on the bar. Everybody is taken to be all in for their stack.
Hands first, then potsUnder the seats, every hand in order of strength: 1st, 2nd, 3rd…, its combo, the pots it takes, the amount and its change (owner, 26 November). Before the river it reads As it stands. Below that, pot by pot as detail: Main pot, Side pot 1…, who can win each, and the Uncalled layer that goes back.
Results name hands, not seatsWherever a result is stated (the ink, each pot, a split, the uncalled chips) the hand is shown as its two cards, never as Opponent 1, 2… The seat labels stay on the seats, where the cards are entered.
Every figure is the engine’sThe pots are packages/core sidePots() as it stands, a split is allocate(), the ranking is evaluate() and the names are nameHand(). Nothing on these phones was typed in by hand.

Open questions

Answers to these change the build. The first changes where the tools live; the second and third change packages/core.

Two tools, or modes of the calculator?The three take the same cards. They could be one Calculator with a control at the top: Odds, Who wins, Pots, keeping the cards when you switch. Drawn as two tools because the request named two features. Recommendation: try them as tools first; merging later is cheap.
Before the flopIs “ahead” the right word with two cards, where ace-king against deuces is near a coin flip? Ranking two cards also needs a small addition to packages/core (evaluate() starts at five).
Folded chipsBlinds and calls from players who then folded belong in the pots but nobody here is all in for them. Not drawn. Simplest: one “Already in the pot” figure added to the main pot, which is right whenever folded chips are no more than the shortest stack.
The odd chipA split that does not divide gives the odd unit to the first seat in order. Tables usually give it to the first player left of the button, which the tool does not know.
“Stack”Taken as everything the player has in the hand, blinds included. Or should the field read “All in for”?
