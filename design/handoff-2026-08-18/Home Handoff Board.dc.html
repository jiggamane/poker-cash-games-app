Every state the club home screen has to survive

The layout is fixed: status bar, group name, the live or idle card, three rows, two dock buttons. What changes is the data. One change from the approved layout — a running game no longer takes the start affordance away, because a club can have a second table going at the same time. Two dock pills — Settings and Invite a player — are now the canonical bottom of this screen and appear in every state, never as a tab bar and never as full-width buttons. At the right end of that same row, one icon button flips the theme on a single tap — sun on dark, moon on light, no menu and no trip to Settings. My stats never previews a figure. Nine states below, dark and light; the written rules per state come next.
