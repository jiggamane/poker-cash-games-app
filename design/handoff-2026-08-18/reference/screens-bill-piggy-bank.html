L1

The bill

Reached from the Bill button in the dock. Total, the rule in force, and the spends.

  • Nothing is calculated here. While the game runs no result exists, so the card states the total on the bill and the block below states how it will be split. Shares are worked out at settle-up, off the counted table.
  • Card right side carries the rule (“split by size of win”) and the count of spends — not money.
  • Each row is time, note, who fronted it, amount, chevron. Tapping one opens L3.
  • Two actions: Add a spend (primary) and Bill rules. Both are admin-only.

L2

Add a spend

One screen. Amount, an optional note, and who covered it.

  • The tags are note prefills, not a category. Food / Drinks / Venue write those words into the note; typing over them is fine and leaving the note empty is fine. Nothing about a spend affects the arithmetic except its amount.
  • Covered by has four cases: one player · several players · The piggy bank · Nobody yet. Several players opens per-person amounts that must sum to the spend; the piggy bank takes no reimbursement; nobody yet leaves the spend unpaid with an amber tag in the list.
  • The time is stamped on save — there is no time field. The header shows the stamp that will be written.
  • Back is cancel. The primary states the amount it is about to write: “Add $120 to the bill”.

L3

Spend · edit

The two-fronter case, and where a spend is corrected or voided.

  • Rows are Amount, Note, then Covered by with one row per fronter and what each put in. The fronted amounts must sum to the spend; until they do the row goes red and Save is blocked.
  • Each fronter is paid back exactly what they fronted at settle-up. Fronting is not exemption — their own share still comes off their result.
  • Void keeps the original line in the ledger and writes a correction; it never erases. The card notes who logged it and that it has been edited.

The bill · nothing on it

Before anyone spends anything. Reachable every night.

  • The empty state says plainly that nothing here means nothing is charged.
  • Add a spend stays the primary; Bill rules stays reachable, so the rule can be set before the first pizza.

L5

Bill rules

The formula only. No piggy bank settings on this screen.

  • Three splits, one selected: by size of win (default) · evenly between the winners · evenly between everyone, which is the only option that charges losers.
  • Rounding granularity is a row of its own; the remainder always goes to the largest share.
  • Changing the rule mid-night changes nothing already charged, because nothing has been. The arithmetic runs once at settle-up.

L6

Piggy bank rules

Its own screen, reached from the house rules — never from the bill.

  • The card states the rule (“Charged on every win · 5%”, winners only) and no total: what the piggy bank collects is unknowable until the night is counted.
  • Rows: on/off for tonight, the charge, and who collects it.
  • Off for tonight is a per-player exception — the filled chip is the player switched off. It applies to this night only and never touches the group setting.
  • The piggy bank is charged on wins, so a player who finishes down never pays into it.
