The poker club · build reference

The group · GR1–GR8

Everything that belongs to a group rather than to one evening: what it is, who is in it, and what the host has set. Admin-only, reached from Home, never from inside a live session. Eight screens in the confirmed chrome, lifted verbatim from the design source — every value is inline on the element, so read the markup as the spec. Frames are 402 × 874; the 11px surround is the device bezel. Written spec: 12-the-group.md.

How a game gets its settings
Club defaultSet once in Settings → Money rules. Currency, default buy-in, bill split, rounding, piggy bank charge, who collects, who pays in.GR7, GR8
Last gameWhat the previous session actually ran with, including anything the host overrode that night. This layer is what makes every night after the first a one-tap setup.carried forward
This gameOpens pre-filled from the two layers above. The host may override anything for tonight alone; on most nights nobody touches it.house rules sheet
Settle-upThe night is computed against the values it ran with, frozen at that moment. Later club changes never reach back.immutable

Consequence for the setup flow: starting a game is adding players and their first buy-ins. Every money rule arrives pre-filled from the club and the last game, the host can open the house rules to change something, and on most nights nobody does.

GR1

Club home · the root

The only screen with no back button. Everything in the group hangs off it.

  • Title row is the club name at 800 30px/1 with a 38px avatar pushed right; the meta beneath states roster size, default buy-in and your standing.
  • With a night running the card carries the green running-time tag, what is on the table, and one primary: Open tonight. With no night running the same card becomes the setup entry point.
  • Four rows only: My nights, Players, Settings, Your groups.
  • Group management is reached from here, never from inside a live session. A running night has its own dock and its own house rules.

GR2

Your groups

The only cross-group screen in the app.

  • Your standing is on every row, because it decides what you can do inside. A live night in another club shows as green meta on its row.
  • Tapping a group makes it the club and lands on its home. Nothing is editable here — a group is managed from inside it.

GR3

New group · step 1 of 3

One sheet, three steps, content replaced in place.

  • Step 1 is the basics: name, currency, default buy-in. Steps 2 and 3 add players and money rules, and both can be skipped.
  • A group needs nothing but a name to exist. Anything skipped falls back to the app defaults and can be set later in Settings.

GR4

Players · the roster

Everyone the club knows, whether or not they have the app.

  • One list, no pending section. Standing is a badge on the row: ADMIN, plain member, NAME ONLY, or INVITED while a link is out.
  • The primary is Add a player by name — naming is the only way into the club.
  • Players can be renamed and removed here. Removing keeps every night they played; the ledger keeps what they already played.

GR5

Player · edit

A sheet over the roster: name, standing, the invite, the exit.

  • Drawn as the name-only case, so the App → Invite row is the live one. For a member that row states when they joined instead.
  • “Pays into the piggy bank” is the club-level switch; a one-night exception is set inside the night.
  • Remove is a red outline, never a primary.

GR6

Invite this player

Replaces the player sheet’s content. Per player, never generic.

  • The invite is second, always. The player exists by name first and already plays, counts and settles; the link only decides whether they see it on their own phone.
  • Single use and tied to that roster row: opening it hands them the name they already have and every night of theirs in the book, and promotes them from Name only to Member.
  • Until it is opened nothing changes. Only an admin can issue another.

GR7

Settings

Four sections in one push: the group, the money, the people, the exits.

  • The group section holds name, currency and default buy-in — the values a new game starts from.
  • A member sees the group and people sections only; money and exits are admin-only.
  • The exits are rows, not buttons, and each confirm is a sheet. Both confirms are specified, not drawn.

GR8

Money rules

The club defaults for the bill and the piggy bank.

  • Bill: split (by size of win) and rounding. Piggy bank: charge, who collects, who pays in.
  • Group default, not law. A night copies these when it opens and can override any of them; changing them here never touches a night already running or already settled.
