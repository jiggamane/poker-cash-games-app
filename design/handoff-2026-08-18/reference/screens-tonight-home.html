H1

Tonight · resting

The main screen. Confirmed, and the only layout for the live session.

  • The running time is the tag. Green dot plus elapsed time in green, counting up from the session start. There is no “LIVE” word and no second clock on the screen.
  • The start time appears once, at the right edge of the title row.
  • Total in hides when it equals what is on the table. Until somebody cashes out every dollar bought in is still in front of somebody, so the two figures are identical and only “On the table” paints. The moment a stack is counted they diverge and the “$5,000 total in” line appears above the seat count.
  • The seat count collapses when nobody has left. “5 seated · 1 out” while someone is cashed out, plain “5 seated” otherwise.
  • A cashed-out player stays in the list. Name goes muted and the amount is replaced by the night result in the green/red pair. Same row, same chevron, no separate section.
  • Sorted by amount in, descending. Tapping a row opens that player’s card.

H2

Player card · at the table

A sheet over the dimmed table. Every entry with its timestamp lives here.

  • Counted is an em-dash until the stack is counted. Nothing on this screen guesses a net before the count.
  • Rebuy is pre-filled with that player’s last rebuy this session, falling back to the session buy-in, then the group default. Where the number came from is deliberately not shown.
  • Entries are oldest first. A corrected entry keeps its line and states the correction inline — the ledger is append-only.
  • Cash out is a normal act, so it is a plain secondary. Only ending the night is destructive.

H3

Table admin drawer

The dock opened. Rebuy and Bill stay reachable underneath.

  • Everything behind the panel drops to opacity: .4 — title, card, list — but the Rebuy/Bill pair inside the panel stays live. A host mid-rebuy does not have to close the drawer first.
  • The backdrop keeps whatever state the table is in: here Dana is cashed out, so the muted name and +1,620 show through the dim and the card still reads “5 seated · 1 out”.
  • Tapping outside closes it. Nothing else in the app collapses an open drawer.

H3b

Hold in progress

Ending the night is a hold, not a tap.

  • The row fills left to right over 1500 ms, copy swaps to “Keep holding… / Release to cancel”, and the dock buttons drop to opacity: .4 — during the hold nothing else is pressable.
  • Release before completion reverts silently. No dialog, no toast, no ledger write.
  • Completing it starts the count-up and no further rebuys are accepted.

H4

Player card · cashed out

The same card once the stack is counted.

  • Three figures instead of two: In for, Counted, and Night in the green/red pair. The seat is closed.
  • No Rebuy button. Cashing out is final for the night; a player who sits back down is seated again from the drawer.
  • The figure here is the table result. Bills and the piggy bank come off it at settle-up, and the screen says so rather than pre-deducting.

H5

Tonight · nobody in yet

Between opening the table and the first buy-in. Reachable every night.

  • Both money conditionals fire at once: $0 on the table equals $0 total in, so the total line is suppressed, and with nobody seated the right side is the single line “nobody seated”.
  • The dock inverts. Seat a player becomes the primary in the wide slot and Bill is disabled — border and label drop to the dim tone — because there is nothing to split yet.
  • Table admin still opens, so ending an empty night is possible.
