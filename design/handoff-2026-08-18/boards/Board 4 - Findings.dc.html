11

What is missing

Read off the flow rather than the file list: places the journey needs a screen and none exists, or where two exist and disagree. Ordered by what the closed-circle test round hits first.
Blocks the test roundOffline and queued stateA three-hour night on bad wifi is in the brief, and no drawn screen shows an entry that has not synced, a queue, or a reconnect. The host cannot tell whether what they just logged is safe.
Blocks the test roundA failed write at settle-upSettle-up is the highest-stakes write in the app. There is no drawn state for the write failing, for two devices disagreeing, or for a settled night arriving twice.
Blocks the test roundStale figures in the E-seriesThe ending frames still draw the even bill split, 57 / 57 / 56. The confirmed rule is by size of win, re-derived in doc 14 as 110 / 31 / 29. Whichever is right, the frames and the doc disagree today.
Needed for v1Corrected and voided entries in the feedCorrecting an entry writes a correction or a reversal, and nothing shows what that row then looks like in the ledger everybody is reading.
Needed for v1Paying, from the payer’s sideMark paid is admin-only. The person who actually paid has no way to say so, and Nudge the table has no drawn message, notification or destination.
Needed for v1My nights, My stats, My gamesThree names, two classified pushes and one confirmed layout for what looks like one destination. Decide which absorbs the others before building any of them.
Needed for v1The player card, twiceT2 (a sheet) and N3 (a push) draw the same content and both are current in their own document. One has to go.
Needed for v1The exitsLeave the club and Delete the club are specified and undrawn, and their hard questions are unanswered: can you leave holding a debt, and what happens to nights other people played in.
Needed for v1Handing over adminSettings assumes admin can be transferred. There is no screen, and whether a club can hold two admins is undecided.
Needed for v1A club-level bookThe navigation model lists a shared ledger of every night as a push off the club. Only the reader’s own history is drawn.
LaterRematchThe settled night offers Rematch. Nothing is drawn between that tap and an open table — which rules it copies, and what it asks.
LaterFirst run, before a club existsThe current club set starts at a club that already exists. The first-run home, and the sentence that explains what the app is, live only in the superseded board.
LaterA night that crosses midnightEvery timestamp in the drawn set is a wall clock. Nothing states what date a 02:40 rebuy belongs to.
LaterCents, and other currenciesSettings carries a show-cents switch and the type rules say whole units only. No screen is drawn with cents in it.
LaterNotificationsThe nudge, a night opening, a settlement coming due — all imply a push notification, and none is drawn or written.
