Flow 12 · across everything

When the app is not in a happy state

Not a journey — a set of conditions that can interrupt any of the eleven flows above. None has a screen in the spec.
OFFLINE, MID-NIGHT Buy-ins queue, nobody is told Affects flows 05, 06, 08. Needs a banner state on N1 and a per-entry pending mark in the feed, at minimum.
WRITE FAILS AT SETTLE-UP E6 says settled when it is not Affects flow 09. The most expensive failure in the app: the night's money is agreed and then lost.
APP KILLED MID-NIGHT No resume state on H3 Affects flows 04–09. H3 shows a live night but not a half-finished count-up or a half-built session.
TWO HOSTS, ONE NIGHT Collector writes while host writes O6 names a collector, so two people can act. Nothing defines who wins a conflicting entry.
Audit

What the graph says about the screen set

NO INBOUND ARROW GR1–GR8 duplicate C1–C7 and G1–G2 with different IDs and a different nav model. Nothing in any flow reaches them. Either they replace the C and G series or they are dead weight in the spec. L1–L6 likewise duplicate B2–B6 (flow 08). G2b (add players) and O2 (add players) are the same task at two moments; only one is drawn per flow.
ONE WAY IN ONLY N10 (correct an entry) hangs off the feed alone — no route from a wrong number on N1 or N3. C7 (settlement due) has no named entry point at all; flow 10 depends on it. E3b exists as a variant of E3 but nothing selects between them; presumably bill-present vs. bill-absent.
STILL OPEN FROM BOARD 4 L1–L6 chrome against the doc 09 test. N3 and T2 disagree on the player-card container. Bill split 57 / 57 / 56 in the E series against 110 / 31 / 29 in doc 14.
