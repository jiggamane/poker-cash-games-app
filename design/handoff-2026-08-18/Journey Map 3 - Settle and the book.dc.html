06

Count and settle

Three sequential steps — count the chips, apply the rules, read out who pays whom — with an off-ramp at each end: nothing closes with chips uncounted, and nothing balances by force.
What everyone else seesA watcher sees the night close and the feed stop. They never see the settle-up controls.
Where it goes wrongThe highest-stakes write in the app, and the sample data in these frames is stale: take the re-derived bill split from doc 14.

07

After the night

The book. Settling and paying are separate acts: the night closes at the table, and the money moves over the following week.
What everyone else seesTheir own history, their own net, their own payments — and only theirs. Inside a club, members see each other’s net for that club and nothing more.
Where it goes wrongOnly the admin can mark a payment. A payer has no way to say they have paid, and the nudge has no drawn destination.
HOSTChases the money.
