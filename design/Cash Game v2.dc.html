Batch 2 · opening and closing a night

Start to finish, in order

Two numbered flows, three steps each. Opening: the game’s settings, who is seated, the money rules. Closing: count the chips, apply the rules, settle. Every step shows where it is (“2 of 3”), the primary action names what happens next rather than saying Next, and nothing is destructive without a count that balances first. Amounts are in dollars at $5 / $5 with a $500 buy-in. Black theme only for now — say the word and the light twin follows.

I can't generate illustrations — these are drawn by hand in code; a real illustrator's set could drop into the same slots.
