The six open questions, answered

1 · The backupA row inside S2, directly above the hold — not a first stage. A stage would make every deletion two screens long to protect the minority who host a private group; a row costs nothing to ignore and is the last thing a thumb passes. Only where something is deleted.
2 · Several groupsA line each, name over outcome. Holds at four with long names (S2, third frame): names wrap, the list scrolls inside a full-height sheet, the hold never moves.
3 · Where S1 sitsIts own group at the foot of Settings, under Account, with a note. Apart from every row that is not destructive, in ordinary type — the red is the hold’s alone.
4 · Closed at 0The count is dropped, not printed: “Closed and kept. Nobody can add to it.”
5 · Where the name is askedAfter the link, for both ways in — one place, so the code sheet is unchanged. Closing the step signs the phone out again; that is what makes it required without breaking Chrome B.
6 · RenamingFrom PLAYERS → You, for anybody with a seat. The admin sees “named themselves · you added Petr” and can put their own name back, which the player can change again. The admin’s account name and their seat stay two names: the account name is read only when the admin holds no seat.
