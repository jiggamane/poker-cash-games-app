Open questions, decisions, and where the brief and the boards disagree

Open questions

Answered on the board

  • Not at the table? Yes. Recording needs a claimed phone and a membership, not a seat. The sheet says “Not playing tonight” on the row so the admin knows the person may not be in the room (3).
  • Passed back and forth, spent once or each time? Once per game per person, the first time that game lands on them. The same game coming back to Ivo later that night costs nothing; a different game that month does. Stated on the confirm (4).
  • Full to take a game back? No check. Taking back is running the game, not taking a new one, and the night finishes on the membership it started with. Whoever passed it, and the person who opened it, can take it back with no gate.
  • Free member holding a pre-membership game? The pass sheet draws exactly as 3: no gate, no plan list, no upsell. They are running a night, and the check only guards taking one. They meet the gate (13) the next time they open a game.
Decisions

Calls the brief asked me to make

  • 2 · Regular admin passing. The drawer row does not say anything about the host night. The drawer lists acts, and passing neither spends nor refunds anything. A Regular who opened the game already saw the cost on O1 (14).
  • 9 · Where the announcement appears. Pinned above the dock on Tonight, or above the Settings pill on any other screen. It is a card in the page, not an overlay. Tapping it pushes Tonight; it dismisses on close, on the first entry, or once Tonight is open.
  • 10 · Does it say the host night was spent? Yes. Ivo never confirmed anything, so the announcement is the only place he learns it: “This uses your host night for September.”
  • 16 · Lapse mid-night. No line on Tonight. The room sees nothing and the admin loses nothing until the night closes. After that, the start row on home says so (16).
  • 18 · Filter, section or badge? A two-way filter on GR4: Everyone | Who can run a game. Everyone keeps Regulars and Occasional as they are. The second view regroups by capability and never prints a tier name, so the word “Regular” only appears where a price sits next to it. If that’s not enough, rename the roster group, not the plan (“Most nights” / “Now and then”).
  • 19 · Members see it? Yes, read-only: no chevrons, no Invite, and a band saying only the admin can pass.
  • 20 · The role mark. The Chrome A meta line under the title, which Tonight didn’t use. “You’re recording · started 20:05”, “Watching · Lena is recording”, “Watching · passed to Lena at 23:10”. The top-right stays empty. The start time moves from the title row into this line: at 360 the current T1 title row needs about 352px and has 320.
Flags

The brief against the boards

  • Hold length. The brief says End is a 1s hold and asks for Take back at 1s. The boards say 1.5s. Drawn at 1.5s so the app has one hold.
  • Drawer rows. The brief lists Money rules in Table admin; T3 on the boards has Seat, Cash out and End only. Drawn from the board, plus the new row. The hint line becomes “seat · cash out · pass · end”.
  • Frame size. Brief: 393 × 852. 08-tonight-home.md and 07-design-tokens.md: 402 × 874. Drawn at 393.
  • Host night renews? Rev 18 §4 says Tier 1’s host night is “single, non-renewing” and prices Tier 1 $4 / Full $9. X2b and the brief say $2.49 / $9.99 and a night that renews. X2b’s Regular line gains “each month” here.
  • Settle is Full only. Rev 18 §4 gates settle to Full. A Regular spending their host night can’t then close their own game. The brief says settlement is identical on every tier. §4 needs to change.
  • X1’s pill. X1a carries a WATCHING pill and “kept by Marek · 3h 17m”. 21 replaces both with the shared role line, so every watcher reads the same way.
  • Light live tag. T1 light draws the green tag in the dark-theme green (#6FCF97 on white). Drawn with the light green pair from N1.
  • Month vs billing period. Copy says “September” and “1 October”. The real date is each person’s renewal date. The build should fill in their date, not the calendar month.
  • Nothing recorded while waiting. In 7 the game belongs to a phone that hasn’t opened it, so nobody records. That’s what the brief specifies. Take back is the way out, which is why it stays on that screen.
  • Not in the system. There’s no checkbox, so the review rows use the plan list’s round tick. There’s no toast or banner, so the announcement is a card with the sheet’s close button.
