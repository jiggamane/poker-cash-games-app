What photographing the flow turned up

Four things, none of them arithmetic

Every figure on this board is one engine call and every one of them is right. What is listed here is what the pictures say about the screens around those figures.

1
R1’s meta line is cut in half
The line under the title on the settled night renders in a 12px box against a 17px line, with overflow: hidden — so players loses its descender and the whole line looks half-drawn. /payments draws the identical component at its full 17px, so this is not the component: R1's body is long enough that the head is squeezed. It is visible only when the copy has a descender, which is why it has survived. Both themes.
Seen on Settled · measured on the same run · not yet in docs/bugs.md, and Screen.tsx is app-wide, so the fix wants a session of its own
2
The balance fact is stated twice, one screen apart
E2's header block states the difference in colour, with the two sums under it. Tapping Next lands on a card that states the same difference again, in a paragraph, with the same two sums in prose. Recorded as finding 1 of the game-outcomes review and unchanged since; the 6 September cut rebuilt the block above it without touching this.

3
Two step counters, counting different things
The wizard says 1 of 3 · 2 of 3 · 3 of 3 over Count up, Deductions and Settle up. The 1 September cut's flow doc says the three screens are Count up, Settle up and the settled night. A host who reads both is told Deductions both is and is not a step, and that the screen they end on either is or is not the third one.

4
Three states on this board have no drawn frame anywhere
E2's amber fourth state — a count that is not finished but whose figures happen to meet — is built and decided and drawn nowhere. Neither is the counted-row animation, which is specified to the millisecond in the 6 September README and cannot be photographed. The block's own short and over states are specified in that cut and marked still to draw.
