08 · Ending the night

Count, deduct, settle, pay

The close is a straight line with two off-ramps: the night cannot end while stacks are uncounted, and it cannot settle while the money does not balance. Payments sit after the night is closed — it is settled whether or not the cash has moved.

Answeredthe host holds the pen
Decided The host marks a payment as paid The pen stays in one hand. Marking paid is bookkeeping the host does as cash reaches them or is reported to them, not a claim a player files. Players and watchers see the same paid / waiting list and cannot write to it, so the Mark paid control is absent from their view exactly as the action bar is on X1. E7 shows Mark paid only to the host. Nothing here touches the ledger or anyone's net.

Answereda name, then a link
Decided A name first, an account only later The host adds a player by name and that name is in the ledger from its first entry — no app, no link, no account. When the host is ready they generate a link for that one person; opening it activates an account already carrying their name, their history and their net. Because the link is bound to that player, there is no claiming, no name entry and no two records of the same person to merge. An account buys one thing: seeing the group from your own phone.
