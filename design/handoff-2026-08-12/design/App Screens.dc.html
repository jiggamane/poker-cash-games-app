What this set now assumes

Four rules the screens are drawn around. If any of them is wrong, say so — several screens move.

A group is sealed Members, ledgers, rules and money live inside one group and are never readable from another. The only thing that crosses is a player’s own stats page, which nobody else can open.
One person, several groups You can host one group and play in three more. Home shows the group you are in; G3 switches. Nothing is carried between them — not players, not debts, not rules.
The kitty has a default and an exception The rate is one group rule — 5% off each win. Who it comes off is a group setting (C6), so it is right most nights without being asked; a single night can switch anyone off before the table opens (O7) or while it runs (B6).
People can be edited, never erased A player can be renamed or removed from a group (C5). Nights they already played stay in the ledger exactly as they were — removal only stops them being seated again.
Membership follows the person Free sees the live night and its own last month. Tier 1 opens the group’s whole history and carries one night as host. Full membership runs a group — every admin holds one, and a group can hold several.

Six things to decide

Each is a screen that should exist but cannot be drawn until the rule behind it is settled. They appear as dashed cards, in place, on the boards.

Undecided What does a cross-group total mean when the groups don’t match? Different stakes, possibly different currencies. Either only matching groups are summed, or My stats drops the headline figure.
Undecided Can a player be removed while they still owe money? Block the removal until it is paid, or keep the debt on the group with the player greyed out.
Undecided Does a new night inherit last night’s rules, or ask every time? If it asks, a short confirm screen sits between Home and New session.
Undecided After cashing out, can a player buy back in the same night? If yes, the totals row needs a re-seat state and the player page needs two stacks.
Undecided What happens to a live night when the host’s membership lapses? Either the night finishes on the membership it started with, or the ledger freezes and someone has to take it over — which needs a hand-over screen.
Undecided Is a player an account, or just a name in the host’s book? Names exist before people do. Signing up later needs a claim-and-merge step — and decides whether old nights count in their stats.
