Home, groups, membership, the group, opening a night

Twenty-six screens, dark above and light below. Home is the root; groups sit above it; a group holds what outlives a single evening; opening a night sets the money, the seats and the kitty.

Three states of the root

Home is the group you are currently in. Every flow starts and ends here, and every screen in these boards is one tap from it. The three states differ only in what the top block offers: begin, resume, or start again.

02 · Groups

Several tables, sealed from each other

A person can host one group and play in three others. Each group holds its own members, its own rules and its own money, and none of it is visible from another group. The only thing that crosses is a player’s own record of themselves, which nobody else can open.

03 · Membership

What you hold decides what you see

Membership sits on the person, not the group, and follows them everywhere they play. Free sees tonight and its own last three games. Regular opens the whole group and comes with one night as host. Full membership runs the group — every admin holds one. Reached from Settings; the locked states below are the same screens seen from underneath.

Decided The night finishes on the membership it started with If the table was opened while the host's membership was active, that same host takes it to the very end — every buy-in, the count, the deductions, settle-up and closing the session. A lapse mid-evening changes nothing until the night is on the book. Nobody's money is ever held hostage by a failed card, and no hand-over screen is needed.
