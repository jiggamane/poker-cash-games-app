## 1 · Where it is and how it is reached

- **`/odds` is a pushed screen** (Chrome A, `docs/09-navigation.md`): the round
  back button top-left, **nothing in the top-right corner**. It is a place you
  stay in, so it is never a sheet.
- **Entry:** Settings › the *Odds calculator* row, under *Rules & rulings*
  (`apps/mobile/app/settings.tsx`). Nothing else links to it yet.
- **Nothing on it pushes.** Everything that opens, opens in place: a player's
  outs grow the player card; the pot odds row grows into its card; the press
  callout floats over the deck and takes no touches.
- **One sheet:** *Share the odds* (Chrome B — grabber, close, swipe down), because
  it ends in a confirm. It replaces nothing and pushes nothing; its *Share* hands
  the picture and the text to the phone's own share sheet. Not built.
- **One control on the status line, at its right end** (not in the corner):
  *New hand*, a 34 pt round restart icon, always live, clears every card and keeps
  the seats. The share icon — *Share the odds*, the same size, live only while a
  result for this exact table is on screen (status state 2) — **is hidden until
  the sharing feature is finished** (the owner, 25 October).
- **The deck is the keyboard.** It sits at the foot of the screen, four rows of
  thirteen, aces first, and never moves; the table above it scrolls. The deal
  moves on by itself the way a dealer's would: second hole card, then each seat
  on show in turn, then the board, then dead cards. A seat not yet added is not
  dealt to. Tapping a dealt card in a place takes it back (the one thing nothing
  on screen says, since the hint went: **open**).
- **Seats:** three to begin with, one more per tap of *Add a player*, up to nine.
  A seat cannot yet be removed once added (**open**).

## 2 · The screen, top to bottom

1. **Title** *Odds calculator*, 32/800.
2. **The status line**, 13/500 muted, one line, with the two icons at its end.
   It says the first of these that is true:
   1. a seat holds exactly one card → `Player N needs a second card`, N the
      lowest such seat. Nothing is counted and every seat shows `—` while this
      holds (B102).
   2. a result is ready for this exact table → `1,712,304 boards dealt · exact`,
      the count grouped with commas; `1 board dealt · exact` on a finished board.
   3. counting → `Calculating · NN%`, NN = boards counted ÷ boards to count,
      whole number. Each seat in the hand shows a bar of that width (at least
      2 %) in place of its figures.
   4. two or more full hands the engine will not take (dead cards left fewer than
      the board needs) → `Not enough cards left in the deck to finish the board.`
   5. otherwise, fewer than two full hands → `Texas hold’em · deal two hands`.

   Any change to a card — hole, board or dead — restarts the count; adding an
   empty seat does not. A result is only ever shown for the table it was counted
   for, never the last table's figures for a frame. `apps/mobile/src/lib/oddsStatus.ts`
   is the function; its test holds the order. **Open:** beside the icons the line
   has about 270 pt at 402 wide, and state 4 is cut with an ellipsis — it wants
   shorter copy or a second line.
3. **BOARD** — five places, 36 × 50. Dashed = a place that takes a card; the
   solid outline in the text colour = the place the next card goes; a dealt card
   is an ivory card face.
4. **PLAYERS** — one card per seat (§ 4), then *Add a player* (a dashed 56 pt
   row) until there are nine.
5. **POT ODDS** — one row, only when it applies (§ 6).
6. **DEAD CARDS** — one place, and another once it is filled.
7. **The deck**, fixed at the foot.

## 3 · Card entry: option D, the press callout

The 4 × 13 deck stays exactly as built (cells about 25 × 40 pt). **Pressing a
live card shows an enlarged ivory copy of it** — 58 × 80 pt, rank 32/800, pip 26
— above the pressed cell, with a 14 pt stem pointing at the cell and a soft
shadow; the pressed cell gets a 2 pt ring in the text colour. It appears on
press-in and goes on release or when the finger leaves the cell; releasing on the
cell deals it, so a tap is still one tap. A dealt (blank) cell shows no callout.
The callout stays 8 pt inside the screen edge on the A and 2 columns and never
takes touches. Options B, C, E and F were drawn and not taken.

## 4 · Player cards: layout G

Every seat, in every state and both themes, is **a card**: rounded 14 pt, one
step lighter than the ground — the `surface` token, dark `#16161A` on `#0A0A0B`,
light `#F4F4F6` on white; the boards drew `#121216`, one step darker, and the
token wins, 10 pt vertical and 12 pt horizontal padding, 8 pt between cards. In it,
left to right: the two hole places (36 × 50, dashed when empty), then a column
with the name line and the figures line.

- **Name line:** `Player N`, 13 muted; then the **word** in 11/700 caps, tracked
  0.8, in the odds colour (§ 5): *Favourite*, *Underdog* or *Too close* — *Winner*
  / *Beaten* on the river. No word while there is no result. When the pot odds
  card is showing, Player 1 also carries a *You* tag.
- **Figures line:** `Win 82.36%  Tie 0.54%`, labels 13 muted, figures 17/600; the
  Win figure takes the odds colour, Tie stays in the text colour. A seat not in
  the hand shows `—`. While counting, a bar replaces the figures (§ 2.2.3).
- **The outs control** sits at the right end of the card's first row: a 30 pt
  round chevron, the back button's fill. **It is there only for a hand that is
  chasing** — one whose word is *Underdog* or *Too close* — **and only when
  exactly one board card is still to come** (the turn, with a result on screen).
  The favourite has nothing to wait for but blanks, so its card shows no chevron
  and no names. Before the flop, on the river and with no result there is none.
  The flop, where two cards are still to come, **is not drawn and is not built**.
- **Closed,** under the figures: the **names** of the kinds of out as quiet chips
  (`Flush`, `Straight`, `One pair`) — **no numbers anywhere**.
- **Open** (the chevron turns, the card grows in place; several cards can be open
  at once): the chips give way to a table, one row per kind, strongest hand
  first, separated by hairlines. Left, 112 pt: the kind name 14/700 and the count
  under it, `9 cards` / `1 card`, 12 muted. Right: the cards **fanned** — 30 × 40
  corner-index faces overlapping by 14 pt — nine or fewer on one row, more on
  two balanced rows.
- **The grouping comes from the engine, never the screen.** For a table with one
  card to come, `packages/core` (`outsToCome()`, `groupOuts()`) returns, per
  player, every unseen card on which that player wins outright, each tagged with
  the category of the player's best final hand, from the same evaluator that
  decides the winner. **All pairs are one
  group** (an ace or a king is *One pair 6*, not *Pair of aces 3 · Pair of kings
  3*). **A straight flush is a flush, unless only a straight flush wins** (the
  owner, 25 October): a card that makes a straight flush goes under *Flush* when
  a plain flush would already have beaten every other hand on that card, and
  under *Straight flush* only where a flush would not win — against a full house
  or quads — because then the combination is what the hand needs. Nothing else
  collapses. Cards that tie are returned separately and are never counted as
  wins. The counts add up to the player's win count. The canonical example,
  A♥K♥ against 2♣2♦ on Q♥ J♥ 3♠ 7♣ (44 cards to come): Player 1 wins on 18 =
  *Flush* 9 (every remaining heart, the ten among them though it makes the
  royal), *Straight* 3 (the tens of clubs, diamonds and spades), *One pair* 6
  (the aces and kings of clubs, diamonds and spades); Player 2 wins on 26. The
  canvas's *Flush 9* was right under this rule. The other case: T♥J♥ against
  9♠8♠ on 9♥ 8♥ 9♣ 8♣, nines full against it — the seven and the queen of
  hearts are the only outs, and they read *Straight flush 2*.
- Kind names, sentence case: *Straight flush*, *Four of a kind*, *Full house*,
  *Flush*, *Straight*, *Three of a kind*, *Two pair*, *One pair*, *High card*.

**Not drawn, and what the build did:** a card that splits the pot is computed
and not shown; a chasing hand that no card wins (drawing dead, or splits only)
gets no control; a chopped river reads *Too close* for both hands and wants a
word of its own (*Split*?); the chevron's spoken name is *Outs for Player N* with
an expanded state; the row labels read *Flush, 8 cards*. All of it is the
build's, not the board's.

## 5 · The odds colour: option A

Six palettes were compared for contrast and colour-blind separation (picture 20);
the owner kept the one on the boards. **The rule, applied to every odds screen
and state** that shows a result — the table, the share cards, the run-out:

- Each hand is measured against **the best of the other hands**: its equity (wins
  plus half of any ties) minus the best other equity, in percentage points.
- **≥ +8:** green, *Favourite*. **≤ −8:** red, *Underdog*. **Within 8 either way:**
  amber, *Too close*, for every hand inside the band — heads-up this means both
  are amber. On the river: *Winner* / *Beaten*.
- **Depth:** `t = min(1, (|diff| − 8) / 62)` — pale at 8 points, full at 70.
  Dark: green `hsl(142, 38+28t %, 64−6t %)`, red `hsl(6, 45+35t %, 68−6t %)`,
  amber `#E8B455`. Light: green `hsl(142, 55+10t %, 33−6t %)` — lightness starts
  at 33, not the 36 first drawn, so the palest clears 4.5 : 1 on white — red
  `hsl(6, 55+20t %, 46−8t %)`, amber `#7A5410`.
- Three or more hands use the same rule: 42.86 / 47.62 / 9.52 on a turn is amber,
  amber, red.
- **Never colour alone:** the word always goes with the colour, so a colour-blind
  reader has the same information. On the share and run-out visuals each player
  also has a numbered badge and the bar carries the numbers 1 and 2 inside it.
- **This is an exception to the app's rule that red and green mean money only.**
  The tokens file and `docs/screens.md` say so. Amber elsewhere means *waiting on
  somebody*; here it means *too close*.

## 6 · Pot odds — permanent

**No setting and no "Experiment" label.** One row, header *POT ODDS*, under the
player cards, **on the flop and the turn only**, while an exact result is on
screen and Player 1 is in the hand. Not before the flop, not on the river, not
with Player 1 out, not with no result. Player 1 is always *you* and carries the
*You* tag while the row shows.

Closed: a card, *Pot odds* 17/700 over *Type the pot and what you must call*, a
chevron at the right. Open, in place: *Pot* and *To call*, typed on the app's own
amount keypad in the group's currency (with a *Done*); then *Needs* and *You
have*, 30/800; a bar with the fill at *You have* and an amber marker at the
break-even point with `break-even 25%` under it; one money line, *Over many runs
this call returns +$16*, the amount in the money colours because it is money;
and the formula in small type ending *Side pots are not counted.*

- *Needs* = call ÷ (pot + call). *You have* = Player 1's wins plus half of any ties.
  The money line = equity × (pot + call) − call, in the group's whole units,
  rounded half away from zero. All of it is `potOdds()` in
  `packages/core/src/odds/potOdds.ts`; the screen adds up nothing.
- **No "call" or "fold" wording anywhere.** The app states arithmetic.
- Departures from the board, recorded in `docs/screens.md`: whole units, not
  cents; a losing call reads `returns −$35` in the loss colour (not drawn); the
  *Done*, the `—` before a call is typed, and the *You* tag on the list are copy
  the build added. **Open:** while an amount is being typed the money line scrolls
  under the keypad; not yet seen on a phone.

  cards a hairline edge. More than four hands is not drawn (the note proposes the
  top four as columns and *+ N more*).
- **The text** that goes with it, and the alt text: `A♠A♥ 82.36% · K♠K♥ 17.09% ·

## 8 · The run-out — settled on the canvas; the one-card summary built

**Built 25 October: picture 19 as `/run-out`,** a push from the calculator's
*Run it out* button, every street counted exactly by `packages/core/src/odds/runout.ts`
(pre-flop included) and held by `ui-odds.mjs --run-out`. The four street frames
(15–18) with the pills and the bar are not built. `docs/screens.md` has the seven
places the screen departs from the board and why — the first being the owner's
own rework of 26 October: one board with the streets marked on it, and a grid
of signed figures, in place of the board repeated under every street.


What the owner settled on the four street frames and the one-card summary
(pictures 15–19), for whichever of the formats is built:

- **A stage display, not a graph:** *Pre-flop · Flop · Turn · River* as four
  pills under the title — the current one solid, passed ones filled grey, ones to
  come dashed — and one line under the cards saying what is left: *1,712,304
  boards still to come*, *990 boards still to come*, *44 cards could still come*,
  *Player 1 wins · a pair of kings beats a pair of deuces*.
- **Both hands stay in sight** on every frame and on the one-card summary.
- **One continuous bar for the two hands** under the figures: Player 1's colour
  from the left, Player 2's from the right, a thin dark line between, the numbers
  1 and 2 inside. **At 100 % the other segment is gone**, not a sliver.
- **The one-card summary shows the board filling in**, five places per street,
  dashed for the cards still to come, no graph.
- **Formats, my pick, not yet confirmed:** an in-app *Run it out* replay first (no
  new package; a screen recording is the video), then the one-card PNG through
  the same share sheet; MP4 later, rendered off the phone; GIF only as a
  fallback. The storyboard timing (about 7.5 s, loops, light motion, steps by tap
  with reduce-motion on) is on the canvas.
- **Tried and not decided:** a quieter frame with no stage pills and no empty
  places, one state at a time.

## 9 · Copy, and what is not drawn

Every string on the screen was the engineer's; these are now the owner's by
decision: *Odds calculator · Board · Players · Player 1…9 · Win · Tie · Add a
player · Dead cards · New hand · Share the odds · Pot odds · Type the pot and what
you must call · Pot · To call · Needs · You have · Over many runs this call returns
… · Side pots are not counted. · Favourite · Underdog · Too close · Winner · Beaten
· Flush, Straight, One pair and the other hand names · N cards*, and the five
status-line states in § 2.

**Not drawn, and flagged rather than invented:** *Done* on the keypad; the `—`
before a call is typed; the *You* tag on the player list; a losing call's money
line; a splitting card among the outs; a chasing hand with no outs; the spoken
name of the outs chevron; what tells a reader a dealt card can be tapped back;
shorter copy for status state 4.

## 10 · What holds it

`npm run check` (the engine's tests and the screen's pure functions),
`npm run check:ui` (`ui-odds.mjs` plays the tables by name and compares every
figure, the status line and, now, the outs and pot odds cards to the engine, at
393 and 320 wide; `ui-audit.mjs` holds the chrome, the surfaces and nothing cut
off), `npm run odds:verify` (every seven-card hand against the published counts
and a second ranking). A design change that renames a control changes the driver
in the same commit.
