Cash game · new board

The book — a ledger for cash nights

No clock, no levels, no breaks. One host writes down every buy-in, rebuy, cash-out and bill with the time it happened; everyone else watches the same list. At the end the app counts the table, applies the money rules, and says who pays whom. Sessions add up per month and per book.

Style Card room Oxblood, bone, brass. 6 px corners, Caslon numbers — deliberately not the tournament app.

Not the tournament app
01Corners are 5–6 px, not 22–38 px. Rows sit on hairlines instead of inside stacked cards.
02Numbers are Caslon, not condensed sans. Money reads like a ledger, not a scoreboard.
03Oxblood and brass instead of dusk purple and apricot. One theme for now; a bone daylight variant is the obvious second.
04Four tabs, active marked by a 2 px top rule — no filled pills, no accent dot.
05Nothing counts down. The largest number on any screen is money, and the clock only ever states a time.
Open
Daylight theme · Group and player screens · Editing a past session after it settled · Whether viewers may confirm their own count
