Turn 1 · E2 Count up · the balance check

Does the money coming off the table equal the money that went in?

Today the block says COUNTED $2,880 of $2,880 — a comparison against the money still on the table, which hides half the sum. The real check the room does out loud is every confirmed cash-out plus every counted stack, against total buy-ins: $2,120 + $2,880 = $5,000 in. Four ways to put that comparison at the centre of the screen, each drawn balanced and off-balance. Sample night: 6 players, $5,000 in, Dana cashed out $2,120 at 23:15.

Shared across all four: the status block never moves position, keeps its height in every state, and states both sums — a screen that only says BALANCED is not checkable. Settled stays a separate, later status (E6, after transfers are agreed); this screen owns balanced.
