The Poker Club

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Support

The Poker Club is a ledger for a home cash game. The person hosting the night records every buy-in and cash-out on their phone, the app counts the table at the end, applies the group's rules and says who pays whom. No money moves through the app. It records what people at a table agreed; it does not hold, transfer or process payments.

This policy says what the app stores, where it is kept, who else handles it and how you get it removed. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

Who is responsible

The Poker Club is operated by the developer named on its App Store listing (the "operator"). For anything in this policy, write to support@pokercashapp.com.

What the app stores

If you sign in

Hosts sign in with a magic link sent to their email address. Signing in creates an account holding:

  • Your email address, used to send you the sign-in link and to identify your account. Nothing else is sent to it.
  • An account identifier, a random ID the server uses to know which groups and nights are yours.
  • Sign-in records kept by the authentication service: when you signed in and the device session that resulted.
  • Your plan, if one has been granted to your account, and when it started and ends.

The ledger

Everything a host records is the content of the app. For each group and each night that means:

  • Player names as the host types them. Usually a first name or a nickname. A player does not need an account or an email address; the host simply types a name and a row appears.
  • Money movements: every buy-in, cash-out, chip count, shared spend, correction and void, with its amount and the time it was recorded.
  • The group's rules: stakes, default buy-in, how shared costs and rounding are handled, and who collects them.
  • Settlements: the final figures for a night and the transfers between players that settle it.
  • Who recorded what: which account was running the game, and when it was handed to another person.

The ledger is append-only. A correction or a void is a new row; nothing already written is edited or silently removed, which is what makes a settled night trustworthy for everyone who sat at it.

If you join or watch without an account

  • Members join a group by typing a ten-character code the host gives them. Redeeming it links the player row the host created to a sign-in of yours, so your history follows you. The code itself, when it was issued and when it was used, is stored.
  • Watchers open a share link to read one night live. The link is a random token, and opening it is recorded so the host can see it was used and can revoke it.
  • Neither needs an email address. The app signs the device in anonymously, which creates an account identifier and nothing else, so the server can tell which device redeemed which code or link.

On your device

The app keeps a full copy of your groups and nights in a local database on the phone so it works with no signal and syncs when one returns. Deleting the app deletes that copy.

What the app does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or sites, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The app asks for none of these permissions.
  • No payment or card details. The app never handles money.
  • No profiling or automated decisions about you.

Who can see the ledger

Access is enforced on the server, row by row. A night is readable by the host who runs the group, by members the host has seated in it, and by anyone holding a live share link for it. Nobody else can read it, including the operator in the ordinary course of running the service. Player names typed by a host are visible to the other people in that group, so a host should type what they would say at the table.

How long it is kept

  • The ledger is kept for as long as the group exists. Settled nights are the group's record and stay until the group is closed or the host deletes their account.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it.
  • Sign-in links and invite codes expire on their own, a code after one month, and cannot be used after that. A share link lives as long as the night it opens, or until the host revokes it.
  • Support email is kept for as long as it takes to resolve the matter.

Deleting your account and your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. It is immediate. Your email address, your sign-in and every link between you and a seat are removed from the server at that moment.

The person leaves; the record stays. What that means depends on what you held:

  • A watcher loses this phone's access to the nights it opened.
  • A member becomes a name again. The host still sees the name they typed and every night that person played, with nothing linking it to you.
  • A host: a group in which nobody else ever claimed a seat is deleted with its ledger. A group in which somebody did is closed and kept, read-only and with no host, so the people who played those nights keep their record.

The one refusal is while a game is running on your account, either because you are recording tonight or because you passed the game to somebody who is. Settle the night and the deletion goes through.

If you cannot reach the app, email support@pokercashapp.com from the address you signed in with and the same deletion is carried out by hand within 30 days.

If your name appears in someone else's ledger and you never had an account, the host of that group can rename or remove the row. If you would rather ask us, write to the address above and say which group it is.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask for a copy of the personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to or restrict its processing, and receive it in a portable form. Write to support@pokercashapp.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The Poker Club is not for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly hold an account for anyone under 18. If you believe it does, tell us and the account will be removed.

Security

Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit. Every read and write is checked against who you are on the server itself, not only in the app. Sign-in is by single-use link; the app never stores a password.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the previous versions stay in the project's public history. A change that affects what is collected or why is announced in the app before it takes effect.
