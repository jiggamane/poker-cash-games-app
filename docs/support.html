What the app is

The Poker Club records a home poker cash game: who bought in for what, who cashed out for what, what the table spent on food and the pot, and at the end of the night who pays whom. It is a record of what the table agreed. No money moves through it, and it is not a poker game, a casino or a betting product. There is no clock, no blinds timer and no tournament.

Common questions

I did not get the sign-in email.
Check spam, and check the address you typed. The link is single-use and expires, so ask for a fresh one rather than reusing an old email. If the button in the email opens nothing, tell us the time you requested it and we will look at the delivery record.
How does a friend join my group?
On the players screen, invite them and read them the ten-character code, or send it. They type it into the app and the seat you created for them becomes theirs, with their history following them. A code is live for one person and expires on its own.
How does someone watch tonight without playing?
Share the night from Tonight. The link opens a live, read-only view. You can revoke it at any time, and it dies with the night.
The count at the end does not match.
The count-up screen shows the whole equation: bought in against cashed out plus what is still on the table, with the gap as the headline. A gap means a chip count or a buy-in is wrong or missing. Corrections are new rows, so fix the figure rather than hunting for an edit button; the original stays in the record with the correction beside it.
Can I edit or delete something I recorded?
You can correct or void it, and both appear as new entries so everyone at the table can see what changed and when. Nothing already recorded is silently rewritten. That is deliberate: it is what makes a settled night something the whole table trusts.
Can I hand the phone to someone else mid-game?
Yes. From the table admin drawer, pass the game to a person. It arrives on their phone as a card; they do nothing else. Everyone's screen then says who is recording.
Does it work without signal?
Yes. Everything is kept on the phone and synced when a connection returns. A night recorded in a basement is safe.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account. If you still host a group with other members you are asked to pass it on or close it first. If you cannot get into the app, email us from the address you signed in with. The privacy policy has the detail.
Where is my data kept?
On your phone, and on a Supabase database. The privacy policy names every provider involved and what each one holds.

Reporting a problem

Email is best. Include what you expected, what happened instead and a screenshot if you have one. A bug that touches money is treated as urgent: the money engine is tested to the dollar against a canonical night, so if a figure looks wrong we want to know the same day.
