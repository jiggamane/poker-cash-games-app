# Running a night on the app

For the first live game, beside pen and paper. Ten minutes of setup before
anyone arrives, and a short list of things not to touch.

**Do this before people turn up, not while they are waiting.**

---

## What is true tonight

- **No account, no internet needed.** The night lives on the phone. Nothing is
  published anywhere until you tap Share, and you are not tapping Share.
- **The paper is the record.** The app is what is being tested. Where they
  disagree, the paper wins and the disagreement is the most valuable thing you
  will bring home.
- **Money shows as `$` even though you play in GEL.** Known, and only the
  symbol — every amount and every split is correct. Not worth a second thought
  at the table.

---

## Setup, in order

### 1 · Make the roster yours

**Settings → Players.** The app ships with a sample club — Dana, Marek, Lena,
Tomáš, Ivo, Petr, and Radka — so there is something to look at before there is
anything real.

Fastest route: **rename them** rather than delete and re-add. Tap a name, edit
it, Save. Add anyone extra with the field at the top.

**Removing is permanent.** There is no undo, no confirmation, and a name you
remove and re-add comes back as a different person with none of their history.
If you are not sure, rename instead.

### 2 · Say which one is you

**Settings → Players → your own name → Standing.** It will read *Name only ·
this is me*; tap it.

Without this the night is recorded against nobody and **My stats stays empty
afterwards**. The setup screen warns you if you have missed it.

### 3 · Deal with Radka

Radka is the sample club's treasurer: she holds the kitty and never plays. The
inherited kitty rule names her, so unless you fix it, **your night's kitty is
collected by somebody who is not at your table.**

Two options, either is fine:

- **Rename her** to whoever actually holds the kitty in your game, or
- **Turn the kitty off** — Settings → Money rules → the kitty → switch it off.

Same for the bill rule ("kitchen & drinks"), which is collected by Marek.

### 4 · Start the game

**Home → Set up the game.** Tick who is playing, adjust their buy-ins, then
**Open the table**.

Two things to know about that screen:

- A player with their amount cleared to blank is **silently not seated**, even
  with the box ticked. If the button says "Pick who is playing" while boxes look
  ticked, that is why — put an amount back.
- You can **add a name on this screen** — it joins the roster for good and is
  ticked for tonight at the standard buy-in. Use this rather than adding people
  later.
- Adding somebody **mid-game**, from the Seat sheet once the table is open, is
  different: it puts them in tonight's night but **not** on the club roster, so
  they are missing next week. If somebody turns up late, seat them — and add
  them properly in Settings → Players afterwards.

---

## During the game

- **Rebuy** and **Bill** are on the dock, always. Rebuy pre-fills with that
  player's own last rebuy, so a player who keeps rebuying $1,000 gets $1,000.
- **Seat a player / cash out / end** are behind **Table admin**, one row above.
- **Nothing is ever deleted.** A mistake is corrected with a new entry — open
  the player, tap the entry, correct or void it. The history keeps both, on
  purpose. Do not worry about a wrong number; just fix it and carry on.
- **Count, then cash out — never both.** If you count somebody's stack at the
  end and they then also cash out through the dock, the night counts their
  chips twice and will not balance. Pick one.

---

## Ending the night

**Table admin → End this poker night**, and *hold* for a second and a half.
There is no tap path to it, deliberately.

Then: **count every stack → deductions → settle up → Close the session.**

If a screen says **"A rule will not settle"**, it means one of tonight's rules
cannot be applied — almost certainly a collector who is not at the table (step
3 above). It names the rule. Open tonight's rules from that screen and either
fix the collector or switch the rule off, then carry on. You are not stuck.

---

## Do not touch tonight

All of these are server-side, all pointless with no account, and all capable of
eating twenty minutes:

- Signing in
- Share / watchers
- Invites and codes

---

## Worth writing down

Two things, and nothing else:

1. **Any figure where the app and the paper disagree.** This outranks
   everything.
2. **Every moment you had to think about where to tap.**

## Known, already logged, not worth reporting

- `$` instead of `₾`.
- **My stats** shows eight invented past nights mixed in with yours — the
  screen is seeded with sample history that has not been removed yet.
- Every row in **My nights** opens the same night.
- After a *second* game, the first one drops out of My nights. Tonight is fine;
  next week is not, and it is already scheduled.
